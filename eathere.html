Eat Here

Concept Project

A community-based app providing insider tips from locals to help groups find the perfect restaurant through a quick, personalized questionnaire.

Tools

Wireframing, prototyping

Around Me

Filter restaurants by distance, price, food type, and atmosphere. Switch between map view (location + tips) and list view (hours, details, and insider recommendations).

Questionnaire

By filling out the brief questionnaire, users can quickly choose where to eat. Questions about group size, atmosphere, food type, food allergies, and time of day help find the perfect restaurant to suit all needs.

Restaurant Page

In a specific restaurant's view, users can see information, insider tips, photography, and their menu. If a restaurant isn't the right fit, it's easy to view other restaurants close by.
