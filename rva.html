Remote Vehicle Assistance at Motional

In October 2023, I was promoted and switched teams to lead the design of Remote Vehicle Assistance (RVA) at Motional. The software we designed enabled remote operators to overcome challenging AV scenarios with dynamic tools, removing the need for human intervention from the driver's seat.

Team Results

Through rapid iteration using agile practices, we expanded the scope of remote vehicle assistance to cover emergency vehicles, traffic controllers, construction, and other emerging use cases, thereby increasing our service coverage. Addressing issues such as vehicle downtime and agent response time, I implemented a priority-based notification system and integrated 3D visuals into our map view, resulting in a significant decrease in vehicle downtime and a remarkable 98% resolution rate for cases within 45 seconds.

Onboarding to RVA

When I first moved to RVA, there wasn't much of an onboarding process for new team members. While coming up to speed, I created a new onboarding process which I used to bring my direct report up to speed a month after I joined. This effort cut onboarding new team members' time in half and was also useful for other teams to come up to speed with RVA.

Team Dynamic

The team dynamic on RVA was a bit intense when I first joined. I promoted better collaboration, communication, and efficiency by organizing team-building exercises, encouraging open communication, and focusing on clear and concise language when conversing with team members.

Organization

I managed a direct report while overseeing various aspects of discovery, planning, research, delegation, and execution. I also enhanced our team organization by creating templates for our design process in Figma and a comprehensive design system, which streamlined our workflows and ensured clarity through detailed documentation. As our team grew, we became more efficient and worked together seamlessly despite the continuous pressure on our team from leadership. I am proud of the progress we made and our continuous improvement.
