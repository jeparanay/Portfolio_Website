Hey there, I'm Julia —

I've spent the last ten years immersed in the dynamic world of design in Boston, MA. In my current role at Product Insight, I'm leading design of end-to-end experiences that span both digital interfaces and real-world interactions. I take a systems-level approach to design, shaping every touchpoint in the user journey and partnering closely with engineering to ensure implementation aligns with user needs and practical constraints.

In my previous role as a Principal Product Designer at Motional, I focused on shaping the future of autonomous vehicles. Working collaboratively with Product, Research, Engineering and other partners, I led planning, strategy and vision on key priorities from concept to completion. Working in the fast-paced realm of cutting-edge technology within the AI-driven vehicle sector, every day brought fresh challenges and exciting innovations. On my teams, I applied meticulous organization, process improvements, and comprehensive documentation to ensure efficiency and accessibility. My dedication to keeping projects well-organized and thoroughly documented was instrumental in ensuring smooth workflows and successful outcomes.

At Motional, I oversaw one direct report, building upon my previous experiences at Accenture Digital Products where I mentored junior designers going through the apprentice program. At Accenture, I worked as a design consultant leading teams in crafting clean, meaningful, and accessible products for diverse clients across industries such as Finance, Insurance, Consumer Electronics, Consumer Goods, Education, E-Commerce, Small Businesses, Public Sector, Healthcare, and Aerospace Engineering.

Beyond my professional endeavors, I am deeply engaged in the design community, taking on roles that allow me to connect with fellow designers and mentor emerging talents.
