The Challenge

Goals often fail because we can't see instant results. We get bored, distracted, and discouraged. One Up solves this by visualizing progress in real-time through AR, making goal achievement fun and competitive.

Visualizing Goals with AR

AR contact lenses seamlessly track your goals, letting you literally see your progress throughout the day—no phone required.

Gamification & Social Competition

After setting up a goal, users can send challenges to friends or random online opponents. The competitive element adds motivation and accountability, making goal achievement more engaging.

Challenge Types

Bet-based challenges where winners earn money

✓ Object-based rewards for winners

Social Motivation

Action-based challenges with consequences for losers

✓ Friend accountability and support

Automated Progress Tracking

Once goals are set up, the AR contact lenses automatically track progress using image recognition. No manual data entry required—users can focus on achieving their goals while the technology seamlessly monitors their advancement.

Key Feature

The AR contact lenses use advanced image recognition to automatically detect and track goal-related activities, eliminating the friction of manual logging and providing real-time visual feedback.
